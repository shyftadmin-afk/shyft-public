2. You need an account

After the introduction, Shyft asks you to sign in with Apple or Google. An account is what lets your library, streaks and settings follow you to another device. The one thing that works without signing in is opening a shyft that someone has shared or gifted to you.

Apple Health stays on your device

If you grant access, Shyft reads heart rate and heart rate variability, resting heart rate, respiratory rate, steps, workouts and sleep from Apple Health, and uses them on your device to decide which moment to suggest. Those readings are not sent to our servers and are not included in your account backup. Shyft never writes anything into Apple Health. You can withdraw access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Calendar, microphone, speech and motion

  • Calendar — read on your device so Shyft can offer a moment before a meeting and understand the shape of your week. Your event titles are not sent to our servers. With your separate permission, Shyft can also add a scheduled shyft to your calendar.
  • Microphone — used when you record a voice note and when you speak to Shyft to describe what you need.
  • Speech recognition — what you say is turned into text using Apple’s speech recognition. Depending on your device, language and the feature you are using, that audio may be processed on Apple’s servers rather than on your device. The resulting text is then used like anything else you type: it can be sent to our AI providers to write your moment or to choose its artwork.
  • Motion — your iPhone’s motion sensors are read on your device, to sense things like whether you are still, restless or lying down, which helps time suggestions. iOS does not require a permission for this kind of reading, and motion readings are not sent to our servers.

Diagnostics can include a screenshot

Reports about crashes, freezes and errors include a screenshot and a description of the screen at that moment, so we can see what went wrong. Anything visible on screen can appear in it, including text you had written and, on the account screen, your email address. Reports carry your account ID; we do not send your name or email address as separate fields. We also collect performance traces, which include the addresses of the requests the app makes.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we show no advertising. There is no advertising SDK and no third-party product-analytics or attribution SDK in the app; the one third-party SDK we use is for the crash and performance diagnostics described above.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not share it with data brokers.
  • We do not send your Apple Health readings to our servers or to anyone else.
  • Feedback you send about a moment is stored without your user ID, in an anonymous feed that is not private. We cannot find, correct or delete an individual piece of it unless you can quote what you wrote. Please do not put personal details in it.

5. Who processes data on our behalf

  • Supabase — your account and the synced library described above.
  • Cloudflare — our API, the usage counters, share and gift links, and storage for the audio and artwork we generate for you.
  • Anthropic — receives what you write or say when you ask for a moment, your search queries, and the short profile built from your past sessions, and writes the script.
  • OpenAI — draws the artwork. It receives only a short description of the scene, written by Anthropic’s model; your own words are not passed to it.
  • ElevenLabs — turns the finished script into speech. If you ask Shyft to re-voice one of your own recordings, ElevenLabs also receives that recording itself.
  • Sentry — crash, freeze and performance diagnostics.
  • Apple — the App Store handles your purchase and subscription, and we never see your payment details. If iCloud is switched on, Apple also holds a synced copy of your library and settings, and your recordings sit in your own iCloud Drive. Apple’s speech recognition receives the audio described above.

One more, outside the app: the web page that plays a shyft someone shared with you loads its fonts from Google (Google Fonts, including fonts.gstatic.com) and Fontshare, so opening a shared link discloses your IP address to those services.

Each of these providers processes data under a written data processing agreement. Some are based outside the UK, mainly in the United States; for those transfers we rely on the safeguards in our agreements with them, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses. You can ask us for more detail about the safeguards that apply to a particular provider.

Which providers we use may change; when one is replaced, this page is updated as described in section 13. This policy covers only Shyft. It does not cover Apple, Google, or any other site or service you reach from a link in the app or on this page; those have their own privacy policies.

6. Our legal bases (UK GDPR)

  • Performance of a contract — running your account, syncing your library, and creating and delivering the content you ask for, including sending what you write or say to our AI providers when you ask Shyft to make something for you.
  • Legitimate interests — keeping the app stable and secure, and preventing abuse of the generation features.
  • Consent — access to Apple Health, your calendar, the microphone and notifications. You can withdraw any of these at any time in iOS Settings; withdrawing consent stops that feature, not the whole app, and does not affect anything done before you withdrew it.
  • Explicit consent — where what you write or say describes your health or state of mind, we ask for your explicit consent before it is sent to our AI providers.

Some of Shyft’s suggestions are produced automatically, including one that can prepare a new moment for you without you asking. These decide which piece of audio to offer you, and when. They produce no legal effect and nothing similarly significant, so the restrictions on significant automated decisions in UK GDPR Articles 22A–22D do not apply.

7. How long we keep it

  • Your account and synced library — until you delete your account.
  • Audio and artwork we generated for you — kept in our storage under a random file name. It is not listed anywhere and is not held against your account, but anyone holding the exact link can play it. We have no automated process that deletes these files; if you send us the exact links, we will remove them.
  • Links you share from our library — expire automatically after 24 hours.
  • Links to moments you made yourself — stay live until you revoke them, so that the person you sent one to can still open it later.
  • Shyfts you send as a gift — the link, your name and your message expire after 30 days. The generated audio behind a gift stays in storage as described above.
  • Usage counters used to prevent abuse — expire on their own, at most after 35 days.
  • Feedback — kept indefinitely, without any user ID.
  • Diagnostics — kept on our diagnostics provider’s retention schedule, typically around 90 days.

We may keep information for longer where the law requires it, or where we need it to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim.

8. Deleting your data

You can delete your account in the app, in Settings → Account → Delete account. That removes your profile, your synced library, your credit balance and your generation history from our database, and it cannot be undone. Anything held only on your device goes when you delete the app; recordings saved to your iCloud Drive stay there until you delete them yourself.

Two things deliberately survive it:

  • The audio and artwork we generated for you. Those files are stored under random names and are not held against your account, so account deletion does not reach them. If you can give us the exact links, we will remove them on request.
  • Any share links you have created. Revoke those first, in Settings → Shared by You. Revoking deletes the uploaded audio as well. That list lives on the device you sent the links from — if you have since reinstalled the app or changed phone, the app can no longer revoke them.

For either of those, or if you would rather we carried out the deletion, you may contact us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}, quoting the share link where there is one. We handle such requests as erasure requests under section 9.

11. Security, and what to put in

Your data travels over encrypted connections, and the database enforces per-user access rules designed to ensure one account cannot read another’s — the anonymous feedback feed described in section 4 is the exception, which is why it carries no user ID. Your sign-in credentials are held in the iOS Keychain on your device.

Two deliberate exceptions, so that links work for people who do not have the app: the audio and artwork we generate, and any recording you share, are served from a public address without a password. The addresses are long and random and are never listed, but anyone you send one to can pass it on — treat a link you have shared as public. Keeping your device, and the Apple or Google account you sign in with, secure is your responsibility.

No system is perfectly secure. Access to production data is limited to those who need it, but we cannot guarantee that data will never be lost or disclosed.

Shyft asks how you are feeling, so what you type or say to it may be personal. Please send only what you are comfortable having processed by the providers listed in section 5, and think twice before including details about other people.

12. Wellbeing — Shyft is not medical care

Shyft supports everyday wellbeing. It is not a medical device, it is not a health or medical service, and it is not regulated as one. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, monitor or prevent any condition, and it does not give medical, psychological, therapeutic, legal or other professional advice. Using it creates no clinician–patient or other professional relationship, and no part of it is monitored by a person.

Shyft’s scripts, audio and artwork include material produced automatically by AI models. That material can be inaccurate, incomplete, or unsuitable for your situation. What Shyft suggests, when it suggests it, and anything it appears to infer about how you are feeling are generalisations, not an assessment of you.

You use Shyft at your own discretion and are responsible for how you act on it. Nothing in the app is a reason to ignore or delay advice from a qualified professional, or to start, stop or change any treatment. If you are struggling, please speak to a doctor or a support service.

Shyft is not for emergencies and cannot get help for you. If you are in crisis, or if someone is in danger, contact your local emergency services — 999 in the UK — or a crisis line. Our support page lists the numbers we know of.
