Shyft helps you take a short guided moment — a “shyft” — to change how you feel. This policy explains exactly what the app stores, what leaves your device, and who processes it. It is written to be specific rather than generic: every item below corresponds to something the app really does.

2. You need an account

After the introduction, Shyft asks you to sign in with Apple or Google. An account is what lets your library, streaks and settings follow you to another device. The one thing that works without signing in is opening a shyft that someone has shared with you.

Apple Health stays on your device

If you grant access, Shyft reads heart rate and heart rate variability, resting heart rate, respiratory rate, steps, workouts and sleep from Apple Health, and uses them on your device to decide which moment to suggest. Those readings are not sent to our servers and are not included in your account backup. Shyft never writes anything into Apple Health. You can withdraw access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Calendar, microphone and speech

  • Calendar — read on your device so Shyft can offer a moment before a meeting and understand the shape of your week. Your event titles are not sent to our servers. With your separate permission, Shyft can also add a scheduled shyft to your calendar.
  • Microphone — used when you record a voice note and when you speak to Shyft to describe what you need.
  • Speech recognition — what you say is turned into text using Apple's speech recognition. Depending on your device, language and the feature you are using, that audio may be processed on Apple's servers rather than on your device. The resulting text is then used like anything else you type: it can be sent to our AI providers to write your moment or to choose its artwork.
  • Weather — to know the weather where you are, Shyft asks Apple's weather service, which means your coordinates are sent to Apple.

Diagnostics can include a screenshot

Reports about crashes, freezes and errors include a screenshot and a description of the screen at that moment, so we can see what went wrong. Anything visible on screen can appear in it, including text you had written and, on the account screen, your email address. Reports carry your account ID; we do not send your name or email address as separate fields. We also collect performance traces, which include the addresses of the requests the app makes.

4. What we never do

  • We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we show no advertising. There is no advertising or analytics SDK in the app.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not share it with data brokers.
  • We do not send your Apple Health readings to our servers or to anyone else.
  • Feedback you send about a moment is stored without your user ID, so we cannot trace it back to you — which also means we cannot delete an individual piece of feedback on request. Please do not put personal details in it.

5. Who processes data on our behalf

  • Supabase — your account and the synced library described above.
  • Cloudflare — our API, the usage counters, share and gift links, and storage for the audio and artwork we generate for you.
  • Anthropic — receives what you write or say when you ask for a moment, your search queries, and the short profile built from your past sessions, and writes the script.
  • OpenAI — draws the artwork. It receives only a short description of the scene, written by Anthropic's model; your own words are not passed to it.
  • ElevenLabs — turns the finished script into speech. If you ask Shyft to re-voice one of your own recordings, ElevenLabs also receives that recording itself.
  • Sentry — crash, freeze and performance diagnostics.
  • Apple — the App Store handles your purchase and subscription, and we never see your payment details. If iCloud is switched on, Apple also holds a synced copy of your library and settings, and your recordings sit in your own iCloud Drive. Apple's speech and weather services receive the data described above.

One more, outside the app: the web page that plays a shyft someone shared with you loads its fonts from Google Fonts and Fontshare, so opening a shared link discloses your IP address to those two services.

Some of these providers are based outside the UK, mainly in the United States. Where data is transferred internationally it is protected by the transfer safeguards in those providers' terms, including standard contractual clauses and the UK addendum.

6. Our legal bases (UK GDPR)

  • Performance of a contract — running your account, syncing your library, delivering the content you ask for.
  • Legitimate interests — keeping the app stable and secure, and preventing abuse of the generation features.
  • Consent — access to Apple Health, location, calendar, microphone and notifications, and sending what you write to third-party AI providers. You can withdraw any of these at any time in iOS Settings or in the app; withdrawing consent stops that feature, not the whole app.

7. How long we keep it

  • Your account and synced library — until you delete your account.
  • Audio and artwork we generated for you — kept indefinitely in our storage under a random file name. It is not listed anywhere and is not linked to your account, but anyone holding the exact link can play it.
  • Links you share from our library — expire automatically after 24 hours.
  • Links to moments you made yourself — stay live until you revoke them, so that the person you sent one to can still open it later.
  • Shyfts you send as a gift — the link, your name and your message expire after 30 days.
  • Usage counters used to prevent abuse — expire on their own, at most after 35 days.
  • Feedback — kept indefinitely, without any user ID.
  • Diagnostics — deleted on our diagnostics provider's retention schedule.

8. Deleting your data

You can delete your account in the app, in Settings → Account → Delete account. That removes your profile, your synced library and saved places, your credit balance and your generation history from our database, and it cannot be undone. Anything held only on your device goes when you delete the app.

Two things deliberately survive it, and you may need to ask us about them:

  • The audio and artwork we generated for you. Those files are stored under random names and are no longer connected to you, so account deletion does not reach them.
  • Any share links you have created. Revoke those first, in Settings → Shared by You. Revoking deletes the uploaded audio as well. Note that this list lives on the device you sent the link from — if you have since reinstalled the app or changed phone, the app can no longer revoke it and you will need to email us.

For either of those, or if you would rather we did the whole thing for you, write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

11. Security

Your data travels over encrypted connections, and the database enforces per-user access rules so one account cannot read another's. Your sign-in credentials are held in the iOS Keychain on your device.

Two deliberate exceptions, so that links work for people who do not have the app: the audio and artwork we generate, and any recording you share, are served from a public address without a password. The addresses are long and random and are never listed, but anyone you send one to can pass it on. No system is perfectly secure, and access to production data is limited to those who need it.

12. Wellbeing

Shyft supports everyday wellbeing. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose or treat any condition, and it is not a substitute for professional care. If you are struggling, please speak to a doctor or a support service — see our support page for details.
