Subscriptions and purchases

What Premium is, and the free trial

Shyft Premium is one subscription on two terms, monthly or yearly, at the prices shown in the App Store for your country. Either plan lets you make up to 20 shyfts of your own each month; the allowance refreshes monthly and does not carry over. Where a free trial is offered to you, the App Store shows it, and its length, on the confirmation sheet before you are charged. If you take one and do not cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, it becomes a paid subscription on the plan you chose, renewing automatically until you cancel. You need to be signed in to a Shyft account to subscribe, because the subscription is attached to your account. Billing is handled entirely by Apple — we never see your card details.

Cancelling

Open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Shyft and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for. Cancelling a subscription does not delete your account.

Restoring a purchase

If you have reinstalled the app or moved to a new iPhone, sign in to the same Shyft account and your subscription is already there. If it is not, open Settings in Shyft, find the Plan section and tap Restore Purchase; the same button sits at the bottom of the upgrade screen. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought the subscription with. A subscription belongs to one Shyft account at a time and cannot be shared between two of them, so check you are signed in to the right one before you restore. If it still does not appear, email us rather than buying it a second time.

Refunds

Every Shyft purchase is a purchase from Apple. Payment, billing and refunds are handled by Apple under the Apple Media Services Terms — we are not the seller of record, we cannot see your payment details, and we cannot issue, approve or refuse a refund.

Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. This does not affect your legal rights. If something in the app went wrong, you are welcome to email us about it too.

Your account and your data

Do I need an account?

Yes. After the introduction, Shyft asks you to sign in with Apple or Google; that account is what lets your library and progress follow you to another device. Opening a shyft that someone shared or gifted to you is the one thing that works without signing in.

Deleting your account

Open Settings → Account in the app and tap Delete account at the bottom. This permanently removes your profile, your synced library, your credits and your generation history from our database, and it cannot be undone. Anything stored only on your device disappears when you delete the app. Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Apple is the seller, so cancel it in the iOS Settings app as described above, or billing continues. Two things are also not covered: the audio and artwork generated for you, and any share links you created — revoke those first under Shared by You, or write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

Getting a copy of your data

Under UK data protection law you have the right to ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, and to ask for it to be corrected, deleted or ported. How to make a request, and the time limits that apply, are set out in the privacy policy, under Your rights.

Common questions

Audio will not play, or stops partway

Audio streams the first time you play it and is then cached on your device — though iOS may clear that cache when storage runs low, which is what the download button is for. Check your connection, then try again. If one particular shyft keeps failing, you can report it to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}; please name the shyft.

Keeping a shyft for offline use

Shyfts with audio have a download button on their start screen. Tap it and that shyft stays on your device, so it plays with no signal. There is no bulk download of the whole library.

Notifications are not arriving

Check that notifications are allowed in iOS Settings → Notifications → Shyft, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb is not filtering them. Your daily reminder arrives at a fixed time, and shyfts you schedule yourself arrive when you set them. Shyft does not send you anything else.

Health and calendar

Shyft does not read Apple Health or your calendar. The only calendar access it uses is adding a shyft you have scheduled to your own calendar, and it asks before doing that. You can change that permission at any time in iOS Settings.

The widget is not updating

Open the app once so it can refresh the shared snapshot, then remove and re-add the widget. iOS also limits how often widgets may refresh.

Sharing a shyft

Links to moments from our library expire after 24 hours. Links to a moment you made yourself stay live, so the person you sent it to can open it later. A shared link is public — it has no password, and anyone who has the address can play the moment, including anyone it is passed on to. Please do not put anything private into a moment you plan to share.

You can revoke a link under Settings → Shared by You — but only on the device you sent it from. If you have reinstalled the app or changed phone, that list is gone and the link can only be taken down by hand at our end, so email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} if you need that.

Reporting a shyft someone sent you

Anyone can report a shared link — you do not need the app or an account. If a shyft someone shared with you is unlawful, uses your voice, likeness or copyright without permission, impersonates you, or is abusive or harassing, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with the full link, what is wrong with it, and how we can reach you. We review reports and remove content that breaks the law or our rules. If you believe content infringes your copyright, please say so explicitly and confirm you are the rights holder or acting for them.

Bugs and suggestions

Send them to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} — bug reports, moments you wish existed, things that read wrong. They are genuinely useful, though we cannot promise a reply to every message or that any particular change will be made.

There is also a feedback option inside the app after a session. That one is anonymous by design — it arrives with no name attached, so there is no way to follow it up. Use email if you want your message connected to you.

If you need help right now

Shyft supports everyday wellbeing. It is not a medical service or a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, it does not give medical, psychological or clinical advice, and it is not a substitute for care from a qualified professional. Nothing in the app should be used to delay seeking that care, and no part of it is monitored by a person.

If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, please reach out to a person, not an app. Email is not monitored for emergencies.

In the UK: Samaritans 116 123, free, day or night · NHS non-emergency 111 · Emergency 999 · Drinkline 0300 123 1110.

In the United States: Suicide & Crisis Lifeline 988, call or text · Emergency 911.

In Canada: Suicide Crisis Helpline 988, call or text · Emergency 911.

In Australia: Lifeline 13 11 14 · Emergency 000.

Anywhere else: contact your local emergency number or your doctor. The International Association for Suicide Prevention lists crisis centres worldwide at iasp.info.
