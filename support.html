Subscriptions and purchases

Free trial and billing

Shyft Premium starts with a 7-day free trial. If you complete the purchase, it becomes a paid monthly subscription that renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. Billing is handled entirely by Apple — we never see your card details.

Cancelling

Open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Shyft and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for. Cancelling a subscription does not delete your account.

Restoring a purchase

If you have reinstalled the app or moved to a new iPhone, open Settings in Shyft, find the Plan section and tap Restore Purchase. The same button sits at the bottom of the upgrade screen. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought the subscription with.

Refunds

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple, not by us. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If something in the app went wrong, tell us as well — we would like to fix it.

Your account and your data

Do I need an account?

Yes. After the introduction, Shyft asks you to sign in with Apple or Google; that account is what lets your library and progress follow you to another device. Opening a shyft that someone shared with you is the one thing that works without signing in.

Deleting your account

Open Settings → Account in the app and tap Delete account at the bottom. This permanently removes your profile, your synced library, your saved places, your credits and your generation history from our database, and it cannot be undone. Anything stored only on your device disappears when you delete the app. Two things are not covered: the audio and artwork we generated for you, and any share links you created — revoke those first under Shared by You, or email us.

Common questions

A shyft says “Coming soon” instead of playing

That moment does not have audio in your selected voice yet. On the shyft's start screen, tap the VOICE pill and pick the other guide. Some moments — the tones and the Little Shifts — have a single voice, and there “Coming soon” means the recording is not ready. New and re-recorded audio arrives without an app update; fully quit and reopen the app to pick it up.

Audio will not play, or stops partway

Audio streams the first time you play it and is then cached on your device — though iOS may clear that cache when storage runs low, which is what the download button is for. Check your connection, then try again. If a shyft keeps failing, tell us which one and we will re-check the recording.

Keeping a shyft for offline use

On a shyft's start screen there is a download button. Tap it and that shyft stays on your device, so it plays with no signal. There is no bulk download of the whole library.

Notifications are not arriving

Check that notifications are allowed in iOS Settings → Notifications → Shyft, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb is not filtering them. Your daily reminder arrives at a fixed time; the suggestions Shyft works out for itself are held back while it detects that you are driving.

Health, location and calendar permissions

Every one of these is optional and the app works without them. They make suggestions better timed: Apple Health for how rested you are, your calendar for a moment before a meeting, saved places for recognising where you are. You can change any of them in iOS Settings, and your Apple Health readings are used on your device and are not sent to us.

The widget is not updating

Open the app once so it can refresh the shared snapshot, then remove and re-add the widget. iOS also limits how often widgets may refresh.

Sharing a shyft

Links to moments from our library expire after 24 hours. Links to a moment you made yourself stay live so the person you sent it to can open it later. You can revoke one under Settings → Shared by You, but only on the device you sent it from — if you have reinstalled the app or changed phone, that list is gone and you will need to email us to take the link down.

If you need real help right now

Shyft supports everyday wellbeing. It is not a medical service, it cannot diagnose or treat anything, and it is not a substitute for professional care.

If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, please reach out to a person, not an app.

In the UK: Samaritans 116 123, free, day or night · NHS non-emergency 111 · Emergency 999 · Drinkline 0300 123 1110.

Anywhere else: contact your local emergency number or your doctor. The International Association for Suicide Prevention lists crisis centres worldwide at iasp.info.
