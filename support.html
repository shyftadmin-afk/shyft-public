Support

Last updated 1 November 2026

Need a hand? Write to shyftadmin@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and Shyft version (shown at the bottom of Settings). We are a small team, so replies may take a while.

This inbox is not watched around the clock, and we cannot help in an emergency. If you need urgent help, see If you need help right now below.

What is free

The whole library of ready-made shyfts is free. Every account can also create three personalised shyfts free, three in all, not three a month. Deleting your account and signing in again within 24 months does not reset them. If we ever add paid features, we will update this page first.

Your account and data

Do I need an account?

Yes. Sign in with Apple or Google so your library can follow you to another device. You can open a shared shyft without signing in.

Deleting your account

Go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, library and history from our servers. What is on your phone stays until you use Reset Shyft or delete the app. A few things stay, such as share links and usage analytics. To have usage analytics deleted, email us before you delete your account. The privacy policy lists the rest and how to remove it.

Privacy

To stop sharing how you use the app, turn off Settings → Privacy → Share usage analytics. To get a copy of your data, or have it corrected or deleted, see Your rights.

Common questions

Audio will not play, or stops partway

Audio streams the first time you play it, then stays on your phone until iOS needs the space. Check your connection and try again. If one shyft keeps failing, email us its name.

Listening offline

Shyfts with streamed audio have a download button on their start screen. Tap it and the shyft plays without a signal. You cannot download the whole library at once.

Notifications are not arriving

Check iOS Settings → Notifications → Shyft, and that Focus is not hiding them. Shyft sends only your daily reminder and the shyfts, breaths and flows you schedule.

The widget is not updating

Open the app once, then remove and re-add the widget. iOS limits how often widgets refresh.

Seeing the introduction again

Go to Settings → Session → Replay the Introduction.

Sharing a shyft

Links to library shyfts expire after 24 hours. Links to shyfts you created stay live until you revoke them. A shared link is public: anyone with it can play the shyft, so keep private details out of it. Revoke a link in Settings → Privacy → Shared by You. This works only on the phone you sent it from, and not after reinstalling the app. Otherwise, email us the link.

Reporting a shyft someone sent you

You do not need the app or an account to report a shared shyft. Report it if it is unlawful, abusive or harassing, impersonates you, or uses your voice, likeness or copyright without permission. Email us the link, what is wrong and how to reach you. We review reports and remove content that is unlawful, abusive or infringes someone’s rights. For copyright, please confirm you hold the rights or act for the holder.

Bugs and ideas

If you need help right now

Shyft supports everyday wellbeing. It is not a medical device or a medical service. It does not diagnose or treat any condition or give medical or psychological advice, and its AI-written content can be wrong. It is not a substitute for professional care, so do not delay getting help because of it.

Nobody watches the app, and email is not monitored for emergencies. If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, please contact a person.

UK: Samaritans 116 123, free, day or night · NHS 111 · Emergency 999 · Drinkline 0300 123 1110

United States: Suicide & Crisis Lifeline 988, call or text · Emergency 911

Canada: Suicide Crisis Helpline 988, call or text · Emergency 911

Australia: Lifeline 13 11 14 · Emergency 000

Anywhere else: call your local emergency number or your doctor, or find a crisis centre at iasp.info.
